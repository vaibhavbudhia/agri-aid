Three modules, one platform

Everything around the farm, in one place

Each module stands on its own — a farmer only touches the part they need this week.

FIELD 01

Community Resource Network

Find and book machinery nearby. Report breakdowns and get matched to a mechanic.

  • Search by type, location, availability
  • Individual or group booking
  • Breakdown & repair matching
FIELD 02

Farm Operations & Finance

Post short-term labour needs and keep a structured record of what the season actually cost.

  • Local labour request matching
  • Expense & revenue ledger
  • Season-by-season net summary
FIELD 03

Government Benefits & Claims

See which schemes you qualify for and keep insurance claim paperwork organized in one place.

  • Rule-based eligibility matching
  • Application checklist tracking
  • Insurance claim + document upload
